THE MONARCH TABLE

CLASSIC LAS VEGAS-STYLE BLACKJACK

OBJECTIVE & PAYOUTS

Beat the dealer without exceeding 21. A natural blackjack—an Ace plus a ten-value card on the original deal—pays 3:2. Regular wins pay 1:1; ties push. Dealer blackjack beats non-blackjack hands.

DEALER

Dealer checks for blackjack with an Ace or ten upcard, then must draw through 16 and hit soft 17.

PLAYER OPTIONS

Hit takes a card. Stand ends your hand. Double matches your wager, draws exactly one card, and is allowed on any first two cards—including after a split. Split separates equal-value pairs with a matching wager; resplit up to four hands.

EXCEPTIONS

Split Aces receive one card each; an Ace + ten after splitting is 21, not blackjack. Late surrender is available only on an unsplit first two-card hand after the dealer has checked, returning half the wager. Insurance is offered against an Ace, costs half the original bet, and pays 2:1 if dealer blackjack.
